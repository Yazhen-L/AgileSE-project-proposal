# Your Person 

### Choose Who You Need. Build a Bond That Grows 🙌.

## What & Why
Your Person is a personalized AI social companion mobile web application designed for college students.

Unlike general-purpose AI chatbots focused on information retrieval, task completion, or answering questions, Your Person does not merely slap an avatar or a specific tone of voice onto a standard language model. Instead, it integrates **preset personalities, personalized characters, long-term memory, relationship evolution, and shared experiences** into a continuous social companion experience. Users interact not just with an "AI that knows the answers," but with a virtual companion they have chosen—one with whom a sense of familiarity builds over time through sustained interaction.

The key differentiator of Your Person is that **the relationship evolves with usage, rather than resetting to the same state with every chat.** Users can choose to have their companion remember important details—such as upcoming exams, personal preferences, or recent life events—which can then naturally resurface in future conversations. Furthermore, every user-companion pair maintains a unique **Relationship State**. A newly met character won't act like an old friend on day one; instead, through the accumulation of conversations, saved memories, and shared experiences, the relationship progresses from "New Connection" to "Familiar," "Trusted," or "Close," with the level of intimacy in interactions shifting accordingly.

This relationship progression is coupled with **Scenario Unlocks**. Beyond free-form chatting, users can engage in interactive scenarios tailored to college life and everyday relationships. As the bond deepens, more private or profound scenarios become available. Even within the same scenario, the interactive experience varies based on the companion's personality and the specific relationship dynamic.

## For Whom
The initial target audience for **Your Person** is **college students**.

College life is particularly well-suited for a product like this. Students often navigate a multitude of simultaneous challenges—academic pressure and exams, internship applications, shifting social circles, romantic relationships, and the experience of living independently for the first time. Often, what they need is simply someone to listen, someone to discuss things with, or just a source of casual, everyday conversation. Your Person aims to provide a **personalized, persistent, and low-pressure** space for interaction.

At the same time, as college students ourselves, we belong to the target demographic; this gives us easy access to real potential users and allows us to gather feedback throughout the development process regarding character design, interaction methods, prototypes, and actual usability.

Your Person does not assume that every college student requires the same type of companionship. Users can actively choose the specific companion role they desire at any given time; a single user can maintain multiple companions, with each relationship featuring its own independent conversation history, memory, and progression.

## How
Upon first entering Your Person, users complete a brief onboarding process. Instead of requiring complex character prompts, the system asks a few simple questions to gauge the user's preferred interaction style—such as whether they want the companion to be calm and understanding, fun and casual, or playful and romantic. Users then browse the "Companion Gallery," which features a variety of pre-designed character archetypes. Each character profile displays their relationship type, key personality traits, communication style, a brief background, suitable interaction scenarios, and sample dialogue, allowing users to assess the character's interaction style before committing to a chat.

After selecting a base character, users can personalize them further. Beyond the default name, users can rename the companion, choose an avatar from the platform's selection, define how the character addresses them, and set specific interaction preferences. For instance, the same "close friend" archetype can take on different names, identities, and forms of address depending on the user. This approach balances the stability of the platform's pre-designed and tested personalities with a sense of user ownership, ensuring the experience feels unique rather than like a conversation with a generic chatbot.

Once setup is complete, users enter the ongoing chat interface. Companions respond to the same questions in distinct ways based on their preset personalities and relationship dynamics. Conversation history is preserved, allowing users to pick up where they left off rather than starting over each time. During chats, users can actively save information they want the companion to remember using the "Remember This" feature. For information with potential long-term value, the character may ask if the user wants to save it; data is only added to long-term memory upon user confirmation. All saved content can be viewed, edited, or deleted in the "Memory Manager," giving users the familiarity of continuous interaction while maintaining control over what the companion remembers.

Each companion also has a dedicated "Relationship Profile." This profile displays the current relationship status and tracks milestones—such as the first saved memory, the first completed scenario, or a specific number of conversations—providing a clear visual representation of the shared history between the user and the companion.

In addition to standard free-form chatting, users can access the "Scenario System." These scenarios are designed around situations realistic for college students, such as "Before an Exam," "Roommate Conflict," or "Late-Night Talk." A "Scenario" is not a pre-scripted dialogue; rather, it provides a specific setting and opening context for a conversation, after which the user remains free to interact naturally. As the relationship state evolves, deeper scenarios may become available; furthermore, the same scenario plays out differently depending on whether the companion is a close friend, a mentor, or a romantic interest. In this way, the user and their companion build not only a chat log but also a collection of memorable shared experiences.

Through this process, *Your Person* aims to offer users the experience of "choosing a person, getting to know one another, and accumulating shared experiences," rather than simply asking repetitive questions of a generic chatbot.

## Scope
The scope of the "Your Person" project is well-suited for a semester-long full-stack web application. Beyond the core AI conversational capabilities, it entails the implementation of interconnected features such as user accounts, character browsing and personalization, continuous chat, conversation history, long-term memory, relationship progression, a scenario system, and user data persistence. Consequently, the project naturally encompasses the full spectrum of frontend, backend, and database development, as well as authentication and third-party API integration.

I also believe the project offers excellent flexibility regarding scope. A core version—featuring a selection of companion archetypes, basic character personalization, persistent chat, user-controlled memory, basic relationship progression, and a set of scenarios—would be sufficient to demonstrate the product's essential value proposition. More complex features—such as automatic memory suggestions, custom avatar uploads, daily check-ins, notifications, or nuanced relationship logic—can be added as enhancements based on development progress without compromising the viability of the core application.
